<h2>Research</h2>

<h3>Undergraduate Research in Experimental Condensed Matter Physics</h3>

<div class="meta">

<p><strong>PI:</strong> Dr. James J. Hamlin</p>

<p><strong>Institution:</strong> University of Florida, Department of Physics</p>

<p><strong>Duration:</strong> October 2024 – Present</p>

</div>

<p>

The Hamlin Lab focuses on growing and characterizing novel crystalline

materials to understand their physical properties under extreme conditions,

including high pressures and low temperatures.

</p>

<p>

I grow single crystals using flux growth methods and characterize them

through X-ray diffraction (XRD), resistivity measurements, and other

property measurements. I also keep up with equipment maintenance and

repairs as needed.

</p>
